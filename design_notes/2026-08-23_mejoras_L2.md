## TRABAJO 2 — D3: pasada sensorial de olfato/gusto

### c19 — The Isle of Whispers (caso más costoso)

Tres anclas, todas nuevas, ninguna repetida entre sí:

1. En la travesía: *"breathing in the boat's ordinary smell of tar and brine and old rope — the last solid thing left to hold onto against a compass that no longer agreed with the world"*.
2. En el umbral de la isla (el ancla más importante — refuerza la metafísica del lugar): *"The salt smell that had ridden with him the entire crossing didn't cross the waterline; the air past the tide line smelled of wet stone and something green he had no name for, as though the sea ended cleanly at the sand and nothing of it was permitted further."*

### c31, c36, c45 — consejos celestes (sin frenar la política)

- **c31**: *"the stone underfoot cold even through the sole of his boot"* (apertura del consejo) + *"the air still holding the ghost of old wax long after the last candle here had gone out"* (Silent Choir).
