# Auditoría de disciplina de POV y tics de estilo — Batch 00

**Fecha:** 2026-08-21
**Alcance:** solo lectura, sin ediciones. Se auditaron los 5 patrones especificados (afirmación certera del narrador sobre estado interno de un tercero; adverbios de certeza sin fundamento evidencial; tic "the particular/frank/loose X of a Y who Z" sin verbo de inferencia; contradicción "no mostró nada... pero costó algo"; y cualquier otro head-hop obvio). No se evaluó trama, continuidad de lore, ritmo ni calidad general de prosa.

## Resumen

13 hallazgos reales en 8 de los 26 capítulos. Los otros 18 capítulos están limpios respecto a estos 5 patrones específicos (2 de ellos — B1C21 y partes de B1C25/B1C28 — contienen tramos de narración cósmica/de conjunto sin un ancla POV humano identificable, donde estas reglas no aplican de forma directa; se anota explícitamente dónde). En general el manuscrito muestra una disciplina de framing notablemente buena y consistente ("Belial judged", "to Camael's eye", "as though", "she took as a sign", etc. aparecen constantemente donde corresponde); los hallazgos son en su mayoría lapsos puntuales, no un patrón sistémico.

## B1C11_EN.md — 2 hallazgos

**1. Línea 25 — Regla 1 (rasgo habitual de tercero narrado como hecho)**
> "He rose from his throne as he spoke, pacing the length of the hall the way he always did when a plan had fully taken hold of him"

El ancla de esta sección es Belial. El hábito de Aamon ("the way he always did") se narra como hecho universal sin verbo de inferencia de Belial.
*Reescritura mínima:* "...pacing the length of the hall in what Belial recognized as the same restless pattern that always overtook him when a plan had fully taken hold."

**2. Línea 53 — Regla 1 (estado interno de tercero narrado como hecho)**
> "The response came before the day was out — a shift in celestial patrol patterns subtle enough to escape most eyes, but confirmation enough for Aamon, who received the news as final proof that the convoy was real and the timing was his to seize."

Se afirma directamente lo que Aamon "recibió" internamente como prueba, sin marco de inferencia de Belial (que no tiene acceso a la certeza interna de Aamon, solo a su reacción visible).
*Reescritura mínima:* "...confirmation enough that Aamon, by every visible sign, treated the news as final proof that the convoy was real."

## B1C13_EN.md — 3 hallazgos

**1. Línea 19 — Regla 5 (conocimiento fuera del alcance del ancla)**
> "A small, silent gesture passed near the enemy commander's position, lost entirely in the battle's noise, and everything the captain had just ordered became irrelevant before his own soldiers could finish the turn."

El ancla de esta sección es el capitán angélico. El propio texto admite que la señal quedó "lost entirely in the battle's noise" — es decir, fuera de su percepción — y aun así se narra como hecho conocido.
*Reescritura mínima:* "Something shifted near the enemy commander's position that the captain would never see, and everything he had just ordered became irrelevant before his own soldiers could finish the turn."

**2. Línea 45 — Regla 3 (tic "the particular X of Y who Z" sin verbo de inferencia)**
> "their retreat carrying the particular haste of soldiers who had just understood, far too late to act on it, that something far worse than an ambush had been unfolding beneath their feet the whole time"

Ancla: Belial. Se atribuye una comprensión interna específica a los soldados angélicos (terceros) sin verbo de inferencia de Belial.
*Reescritura mínima:* "their retreat carrying what looked, to Belial, like the haste of soldiers who had just understood, far too late to act on it, that something far worse than an ambush had been unfolding."

**3. Línea 45 (mismo párrafo) — Regla 1**
> "others already beginning to understand that the commander who had led them here was never coming back to lead them out"

Misma sección/ancla; comprensión interna de los soldados de Aamon afirmada como hecho.
*Reescritura mínima:* "others wearing the stillness Belial read as soldiers beginning to understand their commander was never coming back."

## B1C15_EN.md — 2 hallazgos

**1. Línea 7 — Regla 3**
> "Uriel spoke first, as Gabriel had known he would, his voice carrying the particular heat of a man who seemed to treat hesitation as its own kind of failure."

Ancla: Gabriel. El "as Gabriel had known" cubre solo el orden de intervención, no el rasgo de carácter descrito a continuación, que carece de verbo de inferencia propio.
*Reescritura mínima:* "...his voice carrying what Gabriel had long since learned to recognize as the particular heat of a man who treated hesitation as its own kind of failure."

**2. Línea 25 — Regla 1 (menor)**
> "Uriel gave him the barest nod, already half-returned to his own argument."

Estado atencional interno de Uriel (tercero) afirmado sin hedge.
*Reescritura mínima:* "Uriel gave him the barest nod, his attention already looking half-returned to his own argument."

> "He watched the same silent light move through the field and felt none of the demon's pure terror."

Ancla: un soldado angélico anónimo. No puede conocer lo que sintió "the demon" de la sección previa ("The Path of Judgment"), un personaje distinto sin relación directa con él. Es una comparación omnisciente que cruza anclas de sección.

## B1Cap08_EN.md — 2 hallazgos

**1. Línea 39 (sección "The Distance of a Brother", ancla Gabriel) — Regla 2**
> "One of the singers caught his eye through the open doorway and smiled, delighted, clearly expecting the Herald himself might join them."

Adverbio de certeza ("clearly") pegado a un estado interno no declarado (la expectativa) de un tercero anónimo, sin base evidencial mostrada.
*Reescritura mínima:* "...smiled, delighted, seeming to expect the Herald himself might join them."

**2. Línea 63 (sección "What the Cycle Does Not Forgive", ancla Miguel) — Regla 1**
> "He left Miguel there, alone again with the hum, and did not ask what had prompted the question in the first place, or press for the answer he suspected that silence was withholding."

El "he suspected" se refiere a Raphael (sujeto de la oración), no a Miguel (el ancla de la sección): es la suposición interna de un tercero afirmada como hecho dentro de una sección ajena a su propio punto de vista.
*Reescritura mínima:* "...or press for the answer that silence, Miguel sensed, was withholding."
