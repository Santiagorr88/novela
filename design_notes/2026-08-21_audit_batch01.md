# Auditoría de disciplina de POV y tics de estilo — Lote 01

Fecha: 2026-08-21
Alcance: solo lectura, sin ediciones a capítulos. Se auditan exclusivamente los 5 patrones especificados (narrador certero sobre estado interno ajeno; adverbios de certeza sin base evidencial; tic "the particular/frank/loose [cualidad] of a [sustantivo] who/that..."; contradicción "no mostró nada... pero costó algo"; head-hops obvios). No se reporta trama, lore, ritmo ni calidad de prosa general.

Nota general: el lote está, en conjunto, muy disciplinado. La inmensa mayoría de las lecturas de estado interno ajeno están correctamente enmarcadas con verbos de inferencia del ancla POV ("she judged," "he read as," "a stillness X had learned to recognize as," "as though," "seemed," "struck him as," "so far as X could tell"). Los hallazgos listados abajo son las excepciones reales a ese patrón, no una muestra representativa del texto.

## B1Cap10_EN.md — 1 hallazgo

**Sección:** "The Unjust Verdict" (ancla POV: Miguel)
**Línea:** 43
**Cita:** "Even the officers, trained across long careers to fill exactly this kind of silence with the next order in a chain that never usually paused, offered none of their own at all."
**Regla violada:** 1 (narrador certero sobre estado/hábito de terceros — aquí un grupo, "the officers," no el ancla Miguel). El hábito se afirma como hecho narrado sin enmarcarlo como algo que Miguel sabe o ha observado de ellos.
**Confianza:** media (es un hábito profesional plausible de conocer para un comandante, pero la frase no está enganchada a ningún verbo de percepción de Miguel).
**Reescritura mínima propuesta:** "Even the officers—who, in Miguel's experience, could always be counted on to fill this kind of silence with the next order—offered none of their own at all."

## B1F03_EN.md — 1 hallazgo

**Sección:** "The Confrontation" (ancla POV: Malthus)
**Línea:** 5
**Cita:** "Malthus found Stolas exactly where his own informants had finally placed him, standing at the edge of Threll's household with the unhurried confidence of a man who no longer felt any need to hide his purpose."
**Regla violada:** 3 (tic "the particular/unhurried [cualidad] of a [sustantivo] who [generalización]"), aplicado a Stolas —tercero, no ancla— sin verbo de inferencia de Malthus adjunto a esa cláusula específica.
**Confianza:** alta — coincide casi textualmente con el ejemplo de violación dado en las instrucciones ("the particular warmth of a man who had never doubted himself").
**Reescritura mínima propuesta:** "...standing at the edge of Threll's household with an unhurried confidence Malthus read as a man who no longer felt any need to hide his purpose."

## B1L02_EN.md — 1 hallazgo

**Sección:** "The Council Convenes" (ancla POV: Iofiel)
**Línea:** 21
**Cita:** "Raphael sat quiet near the far end of the table, listening with the particular attentiveness of someone gathering information rather than preparing an argument, while others further down shifted in their seats with an impatience Iofiel had learned to read as a room already anticipating a fight..."
**Regla violada:** 3. La segunda mitad de la frase ("others further down... **Confianza:** media-alta.
**Reescritura mínima propuesta:** "Raphael sat quiet near the far end of the table, listening with what struck Iofiel as the particular attentiveness of someone gathering information rather than preparing an argument..."

## B1T03_EN.md — 1 hallazgo

**Sección:** "Converging Without Knowing" (ancla POV: Selm)
**Línea:** 57
**Cita:** "Selm, though conscious of the scout's position as he had been since the previous night, did not understand that Cassiel had arrived, by an entirely separate path and for entirely separate reasons, at the same hard-won rhythm the island demanded of anyone who wished to move through it at all."
**Regla violada:** 5 (head-hop obvio). La frase afirma, desde la sección ancla-Selm, un hecho objetivo sobre el proceso interno de Cassiel ("had arrived... at the same hard-won rhythm") que Selm no tiene forma de conocer — el narrador sale de los límites de acceso de Selm para confirmarlo como verdad. La frase siguiente ("one following a lesson the ground had spent four crossings teaching him, the other left with no faster answer...") continúa el mismo resumen omnisciente puente; puede ser una elección deliberada de estilo para el momento de convergencia, pero rompe el ceñimiento de POV tal como está definido para el resto del capítulo.
**Confianza:** alta.
**Reescritura mínima propuesta:** "Selm, though conscious of the scout's position as he had been since the previous night, had no way of knowing that Cassiel — judging only by his tracks — seemed to have arrived, by an entirely separate path and for entirely separate reasons, at the same hard-won rhythm the island demanded of anyone who wished to move through it at all."
