## Chapter Review Verdict — B2C36.7 "No One Trained to Hear It"

**Result:** PASS WITH FIXES (all required fixes applied before this report was finalized)

**Reviewers:** 4 Claude subagents (Continuity/Canon, Style/Voice, Pacing/Structure, Dialogue/Character Voice) + Codex CLI independent read (`codex exec -s read-only`, no canon access).

### Required fixes (blocking) — all applied

- **[Dialogue] Third-person self-reference bug** — Jahiel's line originally read *"before Jahiel's had a chance to look at what's under it"* while Jahiel himself was the speaker. Fix applied: corrected to first person, *"before I've had a chance to look at what's under it."*

- **[Continuity/Canon] Reveal-pacing risk on the withheld verse** — the closing line originally claimed Raziel's recovered fragment *"matched, syllable for syllable, the shape of a verse he had already decided"* — too precise, reads as a textual confirmation of the specific verse he withheld from Gabriel in B2C36.5 rather than a resonance/cadence match. Fix applied: reworded to *"close enough to the shape of a verse he had already decided"* and restructured the ending so Raziel is shown recognizing a cadence, not confirming exact wording.

- **[Craft/Continuity — found independently by Codex] "Four words" contradiction** — the manifestation resolves into four spoken words (*"someone will remember this"*) during the fight, witnessed by all three captains, but the closing paragraph originally implied the sealed page Raziel reads contains those same four words while also claiming the captains were "never permitted to read" it — an internal contradiction a first-time reader would catch. Fix applied: added a beat (in the aftermath section) where Jahiel transcribes the *full* spiral inscription onto a page before sealing the chamber (establishing physically where "the sealed page" comes from — this also closes a continuity gap the Continuity reviewer flagged independently: the page was referenced with no prior scene showing it being made). The ending now clarifies Raziel reads *past* the phrase already reported to him, to a second, unremarked clause further down the transcription — resolving the contradiction while strengthening the theme (the captains carried the dangerous words home in their own copy without recognizing what they carried).

### Fixes applied on reviewer judgment (non-blocking, but cheap and clearly correct)

- **[Pacing] Beat 6 POV floating** — the combat beat originally granted interiority to Eshriel and Jahiel ("he understood even as he did it," free-indirect framing for Jahiel's sword) inside what should be a single tight-third POV section. Rewritten to stay strictly in Zaphiel's POV throughout, with Eshriel's and Jahiel's actions described as things Zaphiel externally observes ("not from where Zaphiel stood watching," "Zaphiel watched him take..."). This also addresses Codex's independent note that the action's spatial/physical clarity was abstract — added concrete blocking (positions around the spiral's outer ring, distances to the doorway and the caretaker).
- **[Dialogue] Cadence convergence** — Eshriel's and Zaphiel's lines in the study briefing both closed on the vocative "Commander," flattening their distinct rhythms. Removed "Commander" from Zaphiel's line.
- **[Dialogue] Zaphiel's "merciless executor" trait was narrated, not shown in his own voice** — added a short doctrinal exchange with Jahiel in the caretaker scene ("Mercy was never on the list of things I was built to hand out...").
- **[Pacing] Beat 4 (Jahiel's assessment) ran short of target density** — expanded with concrete sensory/spatial detail (the spiral carving's layout, the scorch-lines, the warmth under his palm) rather than more abstraction, which also addressed Codex's note that the chapter leaned too heavily on aphoristic restatement of the same idea ("old but true, hungry not malicious") — trimmed one redundant restatement of "hunger" language in the aftermath paragraph.

### Suggested improvements (non-blocking, left as-is)

- Codex noted the density of unexplained proper nouns (Codex, Ignotus, Caelum, Helios, Claris, Selaphiel, Iofiel) creates some friction for a reader with zero prior context. This is expected and acceptable for an interlude this deep into Book II — all terms are already established in prior chapters (B2C36, B2C36.5, B2C36.6) and re-explaining them here would violate the house style's "no as-you-know exposition" rule. Not treated as a fix.
- Continuity reviewer suggested double-checking that no Fundamental Law is violated by the caretaker's proximity to angelic combat; confirmed the Mortal Perception law is respected throughout (he never perceives the captains' true forms, only physical effects he doesn't attribute correctly — consistent with precedent in B2C10.5).

### What worked

- All three captains land as distinct on both temperament and combat-role axes (Eshriel's impatience, Jahiel's precision-restraint, Zaphiel's doctrine-first mercilessness tested against a genuinely sympathetic edge case) without requiring narrated labels once the fixes above were applied.
- The structural mirror — Raziel withholding from Gabriel in B2C36.5, now withholding the same category of thing from his own captains — lands without needing to be stated explicitly; both reviewers 1 and the Codex read independently flagged the ending as effectively unresolved-but-earned.
- Beat count (8/8) and word count (2,729, comfortably inside the 2,250-3,000 target) both check out per `expansion_guidelines.md`'s hard target.
