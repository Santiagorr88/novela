## Chapter Review Verdict — B2C36.8 "A Truth Told First"

**Result:** PASS WITH FIXES (all required fixes applied before this report was finalized)

**Reviewers:** 4 Claude subagents (Continuity/Canon, Style/Voice, Pacing/Structure, Dialogue/Character Voice) + Codex CLI independent read (`codex exec -s read-only`, no canon access).

### Required fixes (blocking) — all applied

- **[Style/POV — found by both Reviewer 1 and Reviewer 2 independently]** The line introducing Nymos's second entrance narrated his facial expression settling *"in the moment before Vual looked up"* — a genuine head-hop, stating something the tight-third POV anchor (Vual) could not have witnessed, then immediately contradicting itself by also hedging it as something that "struck Vual as" composed. Fix applied: reworded to *"Nymos arrived at the second summons already amused, and when Vual looked up he read something in the too-composed set of the man's features — an expression that struck him as arrived at rather than simply worn"* — the observation now happens strictly after Vual's own look, inside his own inference.

- **[Style] Two stacked-metaphor sentences** (project rule this session: at most one figurative image per sentence, never two combined). (a) *"...a committee was weighing caution against candor, drafting language careful enough to survive its own scrutiny, and every hour spent weighing was an hour Agares's arrangement had already spent growing roots no committee's eventual honesty would be able to dig out cleanly"* combined a weighing/scales image with a roots/digging image in one sentence — split into two sentences, one per image. (b) The original closing line combined a "carry a lie the width of a world" (weight/transit) image with "arriving exactly on schedule" (transit/timetable) image — resolved by rewriting the entire closing paragraph (see next item).

- **[Style/Craft — flagged by both Reviewer 2 and Codex independently] Ending restated the chapter's thesis instead of landing on a felt pivot/cost.** Agares's line ("the word spoken first outlives the word spoken true") and Vual's original closing paragraph both explained the same idea — the chapter ended on understanding rather than a turn, violating the house style rule that scenes close on pivot/cost/question, not a hard/essayistic wrap. Fix applied: rewrote Vual's closing paragraph to end on an embodied, unresolved discomfort specific to his own established character (his pride in slow, individual seduction-as-craft, unsettled by watching an entire market do the wanting for him) rather than restating Agares's aphorism — closes on an open question about his own identity as a "patient artisan" rather than a thesis statement.

- **[Craft — flagged by Codex, confirmed as a real logic gap]** The sealed parchment Agares hands Vual was never opened on-page, yet Vual proceeded to plant specific details from it — leaving unclear how he knew what to plant, and why a sealed document mattered at all if its contents were never used. Fix applied: added a short passage where Vual breaks the seal alone in his own quarters, skims the rough draft once, and explicitly decides the exact wording is Agares's business to polish later — his own job is only the shape of the rumor. This also resolves the timeline question cleanly: what Vual carries is confirmed as an earlier, rougher working draft, distinct from the polished pages Lucifer approves later in `B2C36pt6_EN.md`.

### Fixes applied on reviewer judgment (non-blocking, but cheap and clearly correct)

- **[Dialogue/Voice] Agares/Vual tic overlap** — Reviewer 4 flagged that Vual's dialogue leaned on the same "considerably" intensifier Agares uses repeatedly, flattening the Commander/Captain voice distinction in their shared scenes. Trimmed both of Vual's instances (to "a much larger category" and "a good deal more"), leaving the tic as characteristically Agares's (and, more sparingly, Nymos's) rather than shared across all three.
- **[Style] Seed/soil overreach** — Codex flagged *"Neither buyer knew they were purchasing the same seed grown in two different soils. Vual doubted either soil would ever compare notes long enough to notice"* as an over-extended personification (soils "comparing notes"). Simplified to *"the same seed, grown to fit two different appetites... Vual doubted the two would ever compare notes long enough to notice."*
- **[Craft] Minor word-choice imprecision** — Codex noted Agares didn't literally "promise" an outcome, he predicted one; *"as Agares had promised it would"* → *"as Agares had predicted it would."*

### Suggested improvements (non-blocking, left as-is)

- Codex found the density of figurative language (seed/root/water/market images) high across the middle sections even after the blocking stacked-metaphor sentences were split. Judged acceptable: each individual sentence now carries exactly one image, consistent with house style's per-sentence rule, and the motif is diegetically justified (Agares himself introduces the plant/root/water framing in his own dialogue in beat 1).
- Codex flagged "Volarien" as unexplained for a zero-context reader. Left as-is: the project's established convention (Deseum, Caelum, Helios, Claris, Aflame, Laberynthus in prior approved chapters) is to use fiched weapon names without inline definition, embedding their function in the action around them rather than lore-dumping — consistent with the house style's "no as-you-know exposition" rule, and the surrounding sentence already explains the mechanism in action even without naming it.
- Reviewer 1's continuity check confirmed no Fundamental Law violations (Mortal Perception respected — Selke never perceives Vual/Nymos as anything but ordinary men) and no weapon-fiche misuse (Deseum, tied specifically to reincarnated souls, is correctly never invoked on Selke, an ordinary mortal; Volarien's use is tied explicitly to "the beholder's hidden desire" per its fiche, not generic shapeshifting).
