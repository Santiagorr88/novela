## Chapter Review Verdict — B3C28.6 "What No One Came Back For" (POV Naamah)

**Result:** PASS WITH FIXES (all required fixes applied before finalization)

Panel: 4 Claude subagent reviewers (Continuity & Canon, Style & Voice, Pacing & Structure, Dialogue & Character Voice) + 1 independent Codex read (`codex exec -s read-only`, no canon context). Word count at first draft: 2,301. Final word count after fixes: 2,368. Target range 2,250-3,000 — PASS.

### Required fixes (blocking) — all applied

- **[Style]** The construction "the [adj.] X of a [noun] who Y" — the exact tic flagged and supposedly eliminated in the B3C28.5 review — resurfaced in a new form: "the unhurried care of a man who enjoyed finding out which" (Gusion), "the particular attention of soldiers who hadn't seen an unfamiliar face..." (garrison soldiers), "the frank, unhurried attention of a man weighing a meal rather than an oath" (Ronove), plus a near-identical sentence reused in the companion chapter B3C28.7 for Phenex. Flagged independently by Reviewer 2 (Style) and corroborated by Reviewer 4 (Dialogue), which separately caught the same skeleton reused across both chapters for two very different captains. **Fix**: all three instances rewritten without the "X of a Y who Z" scaffold; Ronove's line reworked to "He weighed the offer the same blunt way he'd have weighed a meal rather than an oath, no more sentiment in one than the other."
- **[Style]** "Unhurried" used 4 times in this chapter (6 across the pair), including a near-duplicate sentence template shared with B3C28.7 ("she let the silence/offer sit exactly as long as it needed to, unhurried"). **Fix**: cut to 2 uses; removed from the silence-beat sentence entirely.

### Suggested improvements (non-blocking) — applied

- **[Dialogue, minor]** Ronove's "Nobody's told us who to be dead *for* instead" read as verbal wordplay his "animalistic, brutish" fiche doesn't license — flagged by Reviewer 4. **Fix**: flattened to "Nobody told us to stop, so we didn't."
- **[Dialogue, minor]** Dantalion's closing line ("An answer I find I respect rather more than I expected to") risked warmth for a "cold, intellectually twisted" character. **Fix**: changed to "considerably more useful than I expected to" — keeps his interest acquisitive rather than affectionate.
- **[Continuity, minor]** "Three books and however many years later" overcounted Aamon's death by one book (he died in Book I; this is Book III, i.e. two books later). **Fix**: corrected to "two books."
- **[Craft, Codex]** The Dantalion memory-trade ("I don't deal in shapes. I deal in weight") was too abstract for a first-time reader to visualize what's actually being exchanged. **Fix**: added one clarifying clause — memories offered "freely surrendered rather than torn out... reads clearly enough even through Mnemosyn's own touch" — grounding the trade mechanically without over-explaining it.
- **[Craft, Codex]** The closing cost to Naamah ("it would cost her something") was too vague to land as a real stake. **Fix**: sharpened to a concrete, specific detail — she has already decided which memory she'll shortchange him on, and when.
- **[Craft, Codex]** The "forgotten legion" premise stayed abstract — an asset rather than a place with a human cost. **Fix**: added one grounding image at the Cinder Watch (an old soldier still saluting an empty flagpole at Aamon's old muster hour).
- **[Craft]** Trimmed one redundant restatement of "Ronove isn't ideologically loyal, only violence-loyal" (Codex noted the point was made three times in close succession).

### Suggested improvements (not applied — judgment call)

- Codex suggested cutting the chapter 15-25% for over-interpretation. Reviewer 3 (Pacing & Structure) confirmed all 6 planned beats present, correctly ordered, appropriately weighted (Dantalion's beat correctly the heaviest, matching the plan's "harder" instruction), landing the chapter comfortably inside the 2,250-3,000 target — not a case for a wholesale cut.
- Reviewer 3 noted beats 5 (witness suspicion) and 6 (paperwork) appear in the reverse order of the original plan. Assessed and left as-is: the sequence (amend paperwork immediately, reflect on Dantalion's look during the walk home) is causally sound and reads as a deliberate choice, not an accident.
- Reviewer 1 (Continuity) noted Ronove's restraint against his own drilling soldiers ("each of them already bleeding, none of them dead... entirely deliberate") sits slightly softer than his "laughs while killing" fiche framing. Assessed as in-character (deliberate restraint against his own men, not a kill scenario) and left unchanged.

The "Captains under Aamon" fiche header in `personajes.md` is correctly left unexplained in-text; the still-valid captain rank is the legal hook Naamah exploits, exactly as intended.

- Codex's independent, lore-blind read called Naamah's voice "distinct and compelling," singled out "I told him nothing false, either... I've built a long career inside exactly that distance" as the chapter's best line, and confirmed Ronove and Dantalion are "conceptually distinct" recruits with correctly differentiated appetites.
