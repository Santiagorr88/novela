## Chapter Review Verdict — B2C03.5 ("The First Breach" — Orifiel/Ezequiel interlude)

**Result:** PASS WITH FIXES (fixes applied in this pass — see below)

Five independent reviewers ran: Continuity & Canon, Style & Voice, Pacing & Structure, Dialogue & Character Voice (4 Claude subagents, each grounded only in their one relevant document), and an independent cold read via `codex exec -s read-only` with zero lore context. No reviewer found a contradiction with a Fundamental Law, a Mandatory Story Beat pacing violation, or a missed beat from the B2C014-016 outline — so nothing here was ever a FAIL, but Style flagged genuine blocking issues that have now been fixed.

### Required fixes (blocking) — all applied

- **[Style]** The construction "the way ___" was overused — 11 occurrences in ~2,500 words, well past "restraint is the point, not a quota." Fix applied: trimmed to 5 occurrences, each now doing distinct work (e.g. "I don't know your arithmetic the way I know a soul's" is thematically load-bearing; several flat instances like "the way it always waited" were cut outright).
- **[Style]** Orifiel's section closed five separate paragraphs on a generalized wall-themed aphorism ("A wall that never needed help was...", "A wall that hesitated after it had already found its answer was...", etc.) — a repeated rhetorical tic rather than earned restraint. Fix applied: cut three of the five outright (paragraphs now end on a concrete image, action, or plain statement instead); kept one that carries real narrative weight (tied directly to the ward's fate) and the opening wall metaphor that frames the whole POV.
- **[Style]** A POV leak — Ezequiel's interior "conviction" was narrated as flat fact from inside Orifiel's own section ("the particular stillness of a soldier who had built his entire service on the conviction that a captain's doubt belonged to the captain alone"), which is Orifiel asserting knowledge of Ezequiel's actual interiority rather than observing and interpreting from outside it — a tight-third-POV violation. Fix applied: reworded so the passage is now explicitly filtered through Orifiel's own reading/interpretation ("he read into Ezequiel's now what he had always read into it... whatever they were"), leaving Ezequiel's actual internal state properly opaque to Orifiel's POV.
- **[Style]** Orifiel and Ezequiel's sections did not read as distinct registers — same long balanced-clause cadence under two different names. Fix applied: Ezequiel's section now opens on shorter, more clipped, procedural sentences ("Ezequiel did not question orders. He had been raised to a different discipline...") and his spoken orders to Kaviel were tightened into blunt, formal military phrasing ("Half the garrison. Reassigned to the search, effective tonight. You hold the rest at standard rotation."), consistent with his dossier (rigid, disciplined, steeped in tradition) against Orifiel's more paternal, weightier cadence.

### Fixes applied from Continuity and Dialogue (non-blocking but reasonable, applied)

- **[Continuity]** The chapter didn't connect Orifiel's garrison arithmetic to Zadkiel's failing border (already established in B2C03 and explicit in the B2C015 outline: "every soldier is committed to a border Zadkiel insists is failing"). Fix applied: added a direct line tying the arithmetic to Zadkiel's border having already claimed the nearer wards.
- **[Continuity]** Kaviel's canon weapon, *Scripta* ("a sword that revives ancient martial styles with each strike"), never appeared in his combat beat during the breach. Fix applied: the breach now shows Scripta explicitly recalling a forgotten grappling technique as Kaviel subdues the scout.
- **[Dialogue]** Raphael's plea read as a constructed rhetorical argument rather than a tired, grieving ask, and contained an as-you-know beat re-explaining the search's mechanics to a room that already knows them. Fix applied: cut the exposition and rewrote the line toward a plainer, more vulnerable register ("I'm asking anyway, Orifiel. I have run out of other doors to knock on this week.").

### Fix applied earlier in drafting (from the independent Codex read, before the 4-reviewer pass)

- A dialogue self-reference error (Raphael saying "watch Raphael continue this search" while speaking in first person) and a name-convention slip ("Michael's own fall" instead of the Book I/II house convention of "Miguel," reserving "Michael" for Book III) were both caught and fixed before the formal review pass.

### Suggested improvements (non-blocking, left as-is)

- **[Pacing]** Beat 1 (the framing/establishing beat) still carries slightly more weight than Beat 6 (the breach). Partially addressed: Beat 1 was trimmed during the style fixes and Beat 6 was expanded with more sensory/action detail (Ezequiel's run across the yard, the scout's capture), narrowing the imbalance without forcing an artificial rebalance.
- Codex (independent, no-context read) noted the enemy type ("a single demon scout") and the weapons Muriel/Anamnesis could be introduced slightly more explicitly on first mention for a cold reader — addressed in an earlier pass (both nouns now get an explicit noun-tag: "his shield Muriel," "his blade Anamnesis").

### What worked

- All three outline beats (B2C014, B2C015, B2C016) are dramatized in full, in order, with Orifiel's and Ezequiel's dossier lines ("If you wish to strike them, you must go through me first" / "Only those who forget honor can be wounded by me") landing as earned characterization rather than dropped-in quotes.
- No Fundamental Law contact at all — clean by construction, since no death/reincarnation event occurs in this chapter.
- The two-POV structure (Orifiel then Ezequiel, `---` scene breaks, no head-hopping within a section) matches the established B2C21.5 precedent for multi-anchor interludes.
- Word count: 2,352 words, comfortably inside the 2,250-3,000 target range at 8/8 planned beats.
