## Chapter Review Verdict — B1C27.5 ("Sparks for the Numb" — Anael interlude)

**Result:** PASS WITH FIXES (fixes applied in this pass — see below)

Five independent reviewers ran: Continuity & Canon, Style & Voice, Pacing & Structure, Dialogue & Character Voice (4 Claude subagents, each grounded only in their one relevant document), and an independent cold read via `codex exec -s read-only` with zero lore context. No reviewer found a contradiction with a Fundamental Law, a Death-State/reveal-pacing violation, or a missed beat from the 7-beat plan in `content/lore/book1_part3_expanded_beats.md` — so this was never a FAIL — but Style, Pacing, and Continuity independently converged on the same blocking line-level issues, which have now been fixed.

### Required fixes (blocking) — all applied

- **[Continuity + Style, found independently by 3 of 5 reviewers]** Line "the same steadiness her fiche had always promised" leaked the production team's out-of-world dossier term ("fiche," used throughout `personajes.md`) directly into Anael's tight-third narration — a meta-commentary break, not an in-world word. Fix applied: reworded to "the same steadiness everyone in her squad had come to expect of her."
- **[Style + Pacing]** "let the silence after the note fade own its own small failure" did not parse — an edit artifact where two revisions had collided. Fix applied: "let the silence after the note own its own small failure."
- **[Style]** Chained simile: "flinched from her first chord as though it were an accusation, recoiling from brightness the way a burned hand recoils from a second flame" stacked two figurative images in one sentence, the exact chained-image pattern flagged in prior project reviews (B1L01, etc.). Fix applied: cut "as though it were an accusation," kept the earned burned-hand/flame image.

### Fixes applied from minor findings (non-blocking, applied anyway)

- **[Style]** Barachiel's introduction stacked two comparison clauses in one sentence ("like a man accustomed to being listened to, though... as though he would have traded..."). Fix applied: flattened the second clause to plain description.
- **[Style]** Mixed-image clause "a fact dressed lightly enough to be received without the weight of being comforted" combined a clothing image with a weight image. Fix applied: "dressed" → "offered."
- **[Style]** "to call it what it plainly was" nudged an inference toward asserted fact. Fix applied: dropped "what it plainly was," now "to call it the first real thing he had done since the retreat."
- **[Style]** A sentence narrated Ezihel's exhaustion as flat fact before hedging it as Anael's inference in the next sentence. Fix applied: front-loaded the hedge — "her shoulders carrying an exhaustion Anael recognized without needing to ask."
- **[Style]** The second Ezihel scene closed on restored composure ("...before she straightened again, composed, ready for whatever the next round of cots would ask of her"), over-tidying a beat meant to end on cost/ache per house style's scene-closing rule. Fix applied: cut the resolution clause; the scene now ends on the exhale and the head-tip.
- **[Continuity, self-caught during fix pass]** Two stray "tonight" references (Ezihel's dialogue, Anael's line "Lumen has done tonight") were inconsistent with the daytime setting established for that scene (it follows directly from the "this morning" roster scene with Barachiel). Fix applied: both changed to "today." A "second night" reference in the later Ezihel scene was also loosened to "another long night" to avoid implying a precisely countable night-sequence the chapter never establishes.

### Suggested improvements (non-blocking, left as-is)

- **[Pacing]** Beat 1 (routine/method) runs longer than beat 4 (the spark, the chapter's actual turning point) — the setup beat outweighs the payoff beat. Partially self-correcting after the chained-simile trim; left otherwise as a judgment call, since beat 4's brevity ("a small, deliberate closing of a hand") is arguably a deliberate "concrete, not abstract" choice per the beat plan, not underwriting.
- **[Codex, cold read]** Flagged the chapter as somewhat "overextended" — the central idea (care matters even without lasting recovery) is restated across the two Ezihel scenes and the closing round, and suggested a 20-30% cut. Weighed against the project's 1,800-word floor / 2,250-3,000 target and the beat plan's explicit design (beat 3 = confession, beat 6 = closure of that same thread, deliberately different functions), a cut of that size was not applied — the repetition is largely load-bearing for the "vigil without resolution" pattern (15) this chapter deliberately uses. Noted here for the record rather than silently overridden.
- **[Dialogue]** Barachiel's "eloquent, persuasive, immensely social" trait is asserted more by narration than demonstrated in his ~4 lines of dialogue; Ezihel's and Anael's registers converge somewhat (attributed by the reviewer to natural POV-smoothing since we're in Anael's head throughout). Both are minor, cosmetic-severity-appropriate, and left as-is rather than expanding an already-complete interlude.

### What worked

- All 7 planned beats present, in order, each a real scene (none compressed into a throwaway line) — verified directly against `book1_part3_expanded_beats.md`.
- Real category-5 dialogue (Ezihel's confession scene) confirmed substantive by both the Claude reviewer and independently by Codex, not plot-transmission dressed as conversation.
- No head-hopping: POV stays tight-third Anael throughout; Barachiel's and Ezihel's interior states are consistently framed as Anael's inference/observation, never asserted narrator fact.
- Ezihel's private crack (steady-under-pressure fiche, momentarily vulnerable) is handled correctly — only surfaces one-on-one with her own captain, never in front of the wounded or other squad members, and she recomposes before returning to the wards.
- Direct, deliberate continuity payoff: the chapter dramatizes exactly what B1C28's single existing line about Anael compresses ("moved among the soul-wounded... leaving small sparks of feeling in hands too numb to ask for them"), echoed almost verbatim in the closing line, and reuses Barachiel (already present in that same B1C28 scene) rather than inventing a new messenger.
- Word count: 2,254 words (`wc -w`), inside the project's 1,800-word interlude floor and close to the 2,250-3,000 target range — no padding needed to clear the floor.
