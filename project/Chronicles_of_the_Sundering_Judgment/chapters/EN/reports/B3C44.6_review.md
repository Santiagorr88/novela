### Required fixes (blocking) — all applied

- **[Dialogue, corroborated by an independent continuity-reviewer observation and confirmed by the dialogue reviewer against the actual lines]** Vine — fiched "instinctive, savage, unpredictable" — read as measured and philosophical in his spoken lines, almost indistinguishable in cadence from Valefar's precise, self-qualifying register, even though his field actions (breaking a charging pack-leader's foreleg) were already properly savage. Evidence: "I'll come. Not for your locks. Because I'd rather hear the answer myself than get it secondhand from whichever of us is more convincing" and the closer "...which is to say, never comfortably, and always a little too late to matter" both carried Valefar-style rhetorical hedges. **Fix**: tightened three lines toward a blunter, physical register — "I'll come. Not for your locks — for my own ears"; "I don't care if it's trusted. I care if I've got something to hunt... Rest of it, I'll worry about when it bites me — same as you"; "I feel a pack that hasn't fought in a season. Not the same complaint as yours" (dropped the observational tag on Valefar's posture).

- **[Continuity]** No blocking canon contradictions found — confirmed clean on the Belial-dissolution rule (no live degraded Belial), consistent with B3C44 and the sibling interlude B3C44.5's "Foras marched a fourth time this week" detail, and Vepar's "Acceptable. For now." tic independently re-verified against its real source (B3C23 line 45 — the "he" in that line is Vepar responding to Gabriel, not Gabriel himself, resolving an initial misreading by one reviewer).
