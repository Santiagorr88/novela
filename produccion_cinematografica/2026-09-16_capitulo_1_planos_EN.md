## SCENE 2 — The Council's Plea (shots 14-28, Miguel/Gabriel dialogue)

Dramatized with the characters' own voices. Shot/reverse-shot.

**14 — "The Chord of Harmony"**
Visual: Soft blue-silver light entering from the edge of frame over Miguel seen from behind; @Gabriel appearing, wings folding against the desert gloom.
Camera: static, faint drift.
Sound: a clean chord of harmony cuts the silence; desert wind underneath.

**15 — "Relief and Shame"**
Visual: Close-up on @Miguel, an expression caught between physical relief and shame at being seen like this.
Camera: very slow push-in.
Sound: wind fading under Gabriel's glow.

**16 — "The Council Is Concerned"**
Visual: Close-up on @Gabriel, warm, contained worry.
Camera: static.
Sound: low harmonic chord, faint wind.
Dialogue: Gabriel says: "The Council is concerned, Miguel."

**17 — "The Council's Fear"**
Visual: Same framing as shot 16, the strain of a plea already audible.
Dialogue: Gabriel says: "They fear this is a deception, a lure the enemy crafted to draw you out. Your absence puts fear in the Host."

**18 — "They Think Me a Fool"**
Visual: Reverse-shot, close-up on @Miguel, voice rough with disuse.
Dialogue: Miguel says: "They think me a fool. Or a traitor."

**19 — "The Sealed Parchment"**
Visual: Medium shot of @Gabriel; as he shifts his weight, a wax-sealed parchment is briefly visible against his ribs, hidden again an instant later.
Camera: slight lateral shift as Gabriel repositions.
Sound: soft rustle of fabric.
Dialogue: Gabriel says: "They think you are their general."

**20 — "From Envoy to Brother"**
Visual: Close-up on @Miguel, his jaw loosening for one instant.
Camera: static, minimal.
Sound: the harmony fades almost to silence.

**21 — Flashback, Onyx Gates (Entry)**
Visual: @Miguel and @Gabriel in the Burning Fortress collapsing — black stone columns cracking and sinking into darkness, fissures of infernal fire, sulfur smoke. Gabriel kneeling beside fallen Miguel, cauterizing the wing with his own light (use Miguel's approved charred/smoldering-wing variant).
Camera: unstable handheld, hidden cut entering on a flash of light.
Sound: distant structural groaning, fire crackling, muffled.

**22 — Flashback, Gabriel's Line**
Visual: Close-up on @Gabriel in the flashback, same design, warm light of the cauterization.
Dialogue: Gabriel says: "Because you don't yell for help in the voice you use for people you love. You go yourself."

**23 — Return to the Present**
Visual: Close-up on @Gabriel's hand, now back in Serephis's cold light, rising, hesitating, and falling back to his side without completing the gesture.
Camera: hidden cut symmetrical to the flashback's entry.
Sound: the desert wind returns.

**24 — "Come Home"**
Visual: Close-up on @Gabriel, maximum warmth.
Dialogue: Gabriel says: "Come home. Whatever this wound is, we face it together. As we always have."

**25 — Miguel's Doubt**
Visual: Close-up on @Miguel, visible conflict, no dialogue.
Camera: static.
Sound: near total silence.

**26 — "I Cannot"**
Visual: @Miguel looks down at the buried road's stones beneath his feet, then looks up and meets Gabriel's eyes for the first time since he arrived — a terrible, crystalline resolve.
Dialogue: Miguel says: "I cannot. This call is a command more fundamental than any the Council can give me. It is a truth I must find, or I am nothing."

**27 — Gabriel's Farewell**
Visual: Close-up on @Gabriel, his light dimming, a single tear of light tracing his cheek, head inclining.
Dialogue: Gabriel says: "May you find what you seek, brother."

**28 — Miguel Alone**
Visual: Medium shot, @Miguel looking at the empty space where Gabriel stood; the desert's heat and silence return, heavier than before.
Camera: static, slight zoom-out.
Sound: the wind returns, more present than ever.

## SCENE 3 — March Toward the Grove (shots 29-31, transition)

**29 — "One Step, Another World"**
Visual: Extreme close-up on @Miguel's boot crossing, in a single step, from hot sand/glass to cool green moss — the cut happens on the footstep itself.
Camera: hidden cut on the step's motion.
Sound: the desert's dry wind cuts abruptly to a still, metallic silence.

**30 — "The Grove"**
Visual: Wide shot revealing the Grove in full — perfect rows of identical pale pewter-metal trees, thin metal-leaf foliage, dull green moss underfoot, even light with no shadows anywhere; @Miguel small and alone at the threshold.
Camera: slow lateral reveal as Miguel steps deeper in.
Sound: near total silence; each footstep sounds abnormally loud.

**31 — "Ambush"**
Visual: Close-up on @Miguel's face, wary, scanning the surroundings with tactical instinct.
Camera: static.
Sound: tense silence.

## SCENE 4 — The Sentinel (shots 32-34)

Character: `@Pewter_Sentinel` (tall silhouette of pale silver mist and pewter-toned vapor, smooth featureless face with two faint cold-white eyes, no conventional clothing).

**32 — "A Flaw in the Light"**
Visual: One of the metal trees, some forty paces off, no longer matches the angle of the others — @Miguel turns and finds the Pewter Sentinel, tall as the trees, smooth featureless face, two dim eyes glowing from the mist.
Camera: slight zoom-in as the Sentinel is revealed.
Sound: absolute silence, no wind.

**33 — "I Have Come for What Is Mine"**
Visual: Medium shot, @Miguel facing the Pewter Sentinel.
Dialogue: Miguel says: "I have come for what is mine."

**34 — "The Sentinel Turns"**
Visual: The Pewter Sentinel turns smooth and soundless to keep facing @Miguel as he walks past, like a compass needle.
Camera: slow tracking shot alongside Miguel, the Sentinel pivoting in the background/side of frame.
Sound: silence, only Miguel's footsteps.

## SCENE 5 — The Ash Tree and Solmire (shots 35-38, chapter close — one-take)

Location: The Ash Tree Clearing. Prop: Solmire (white/glacial version — continuous light, no sparks or flames).

**35 — Opening Frame of the Push-In**
Visual: @Miguel standing at the edge of the clearing, seeing the great ash tree for the first time in the distance — bark like cooled black lava, roots resting in a pool of static silver light; driven into the trunk, Solmire, living white-glacial light.
Camera: wide-to-medium shot, Miguel small against the tree.
Sound: the low, cold hum of the clearing, library-like silence.

**36 — Continuous Push-In (Intermediate Medium Shot)**
Visual: The camera has advanced; Solmire now fills more of the frame, its light reflecting in the silver pool.
Camera: continuation of shot 35's push-in (chain via Frames: shot 35's end frame = shot 36's start frame).
Sound: the hum grows slightly in intensity.

**37 — Closing Frame of the Push-In — The Trembling Hand**
Visual: Extreme close-up on @Miguel's gauntleted hand, trembling, inches from the hilt; his face barely visible at the edge of frame, an expression caught between hunger and dread.
Camera: end of the push-in chained from shot 36.
Sound: the hum becomes an almost physical vibration, resonating beneath his ribs.

**38 — The Forest Vanishes (Cliffhanger, Chapter Close)**
Visual: The instant skin touches hilt, a white-glacial flash consumes the frame entirely.
Camera: hard cut on the flash — the only deliberate visible cut of the whole chapter, matching the narrative "fade to white."
Sound: the hum cuts sharply to total silence.
