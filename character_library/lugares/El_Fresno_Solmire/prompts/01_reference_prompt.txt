Create a dramatic, centered landscape illustration of a single colossal ash tree in a dark epic fantasy setting, standing at the heart of an uncanny metallic grove. No people, no creatures, no figures of any kind anywhere in frame — this is a pure environment reference plate.

THE TREE: An enormous, ancient ash tree utterly unlike the pale metallic trees surrounding it (that context is implied by scale, not necessarily shown in frame). The tree dominates the frame vertically, its lowest thick roots plunging into the ground and into a pool of water at its base.

THE POOL: Where the roots meet the ground, they drink from a still pool of water that glows with its own static, unmoving light — a smooth, flat, silvery-white luminance with absolutely no ripples, waves, or dynamic reflections on its surface, as if time itself has stopped over the water. ATMOSPHERE: Total stillness and silence, no wind, no mist, no floating particles, no motion of any kind.

COMPOSITION: Vertical or centered composition, slight low angle looking up to emphasize the tree's immense scale, the sword clearly visible and readable in the trunk, the glowing pool visible at the base among the roots.
