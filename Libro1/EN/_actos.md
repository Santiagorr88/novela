# Libro I — Act structure (compilation spec)

Each act opens with a title page: act number + Codex epigraph, chapter title on the following page. Applies to print/ebook/PDF compilation and, when audio production resumes, as spoken interludes.

---

## ACT I — The Call (chapters 1-5)

> *Three were forged before the chain.*
> *One laughed, one wept, one waited.*
> *Their voices met only once, and that single chord broke the world in two.*
> *Now the chain frays. If they do not sing together again — by choice, this time — the silence after will not be peace.*

— Codex Mortalis, fragment I *(verse by the author, canon)*

## ACT II — The Debt (chapters 6-21)

> *Pain is not punishment.*
> *Pain is the memory of order, carried by whoever holds it.*
> *But the hand that steals what it cannot bear*
> *will find that memory does not forget the thief.*

— Codex Mortalis, Fragment IV: Lamentum's Curse *(canon, prompt_universo)*

## ACT III — The War Nobody Declares (chapters 22-43)

> *The blade does not wound. It unmakes.*
> *What it touches does not bleed — it forgets it was ever there.*
> *Even a god, struck once, would have to be told he had existed,*
> *by someone who still remembered.*

— Codex Mortalis, Fragment II: The Echo of Solmire *(canon, prompt_universo)*

## ACT IV — The Exception (chapters 44-51)

> *When the king who never fights takes the field, the war is already decided.*
> *What falls will rise unremembering.*
> *The sword knows how to wait.*

— Codex Mortalis, uncatalogued verse *(approved canon)*
